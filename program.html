The Executive Performance Index

Five tests. Here's what counts.

TEST 1
6-minute walk
Distance in 6 minutes on the same course. No running.
TEST 2
Farmer's walk
Distance in 60 seconds carrying one-third of your Day 1 bodyweight in total, rounded down to the nearest 10 lb and split between both hands. Same load at every retest.
TEST 3
Resting heart rate
A 7-morning average, same method and device. Lower is better.
TEST 4
Air squats
Reps in 1 minute on video. Each rep reaches at least a 90-degree knee bend, then a full stand.
TEST 5
Strict push-ups
Counted reps in one set on video: one second down, one second up. Fewer than 5 floor reps on Day 1 means a kitchen-height counter through Day 90.
THE STANDARD
10% for everyone
No fitness-level tiers. The guarantee looks for at least 3 passes by Day 90.

A pass is 10% better than Day 1. For resting heart rate, better means lower. Rep and distance targets round up; heart rate targets round down. A zero rep score needs at least 1 rep to pass. You get the full form and stop rules before testing. It's a performance scorecard, not a medical assessment.

Performance Guarantee
Fewer than 3 passes by Day 90? 30 more days free.

Complete at least 90% of your sessions (Floor weeks included), every check-in and your baseline testing. If fewer than 3 of your 5 Index tests have hit their pass marks by Day 90, I coach you 30 more days at no charge. A pass is at least 10% improvement from Day 1, the same for every client. If screening shows a test isn't safe for you, it isn't performed or counted as a pass, and you'll know this before you pay. You must be cleared for at least four of the five tests to enroll. The pass requirement remains three. If an injury prevents a retest, we can pause for up to 30 days, if needed. Billing pauses too, with no extra payments added. The 90-day clock stops, then resumes with the same days remaining. If you're still short after those 30 days, we hold a final review call to decide next steps together.

If you still can't retest: If you still can't retest after the 30-day pause, we score the tests you can safely take. If you pass at least three, no free time is owed. If you pass fewer than three and have met the participation requirements, you get 30 free coaching days to use once cleared, within 6 months after the 30-day pause ends. No cash refund applies under this guarantee.

No result is promised. Your part: at least 90% of sessions, every check-in and your baseline testing.
