The Executive Performance Index

Five tests.
Here's what counts.

  • 6-minute walkDistance in 6 minutes on the same course. No running.
  • Farmer's walkDistance in 60 seconds carrying one-third of your Day 1 bodyweight in total, rounded down to the nearest 10 lb and split between both hands. Same load at every retest.
  • Resting heart rateA 7-morning average, same method and device. Lower is better.
  • Air squatsReps in 1 minute on video. Each rep reaches at least a 90-degree knee bend, then a full stand.
  • Strict push-upsCounted reps in one set on video: one second down, one second up. Fewer than 5 floor reps on Day 1 means a kitchen-height counter through Day 90.

A pass is 10% better than Day 1. For resting heart rate, better means lower. Rep and distance targets round up; heart rate targets round down. A zero rep score needs at least 1 rep to pass. You get the full form and stop rules before testing. It's a performance scorecard, not a medical assessment.
